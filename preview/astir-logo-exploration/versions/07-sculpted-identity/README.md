## The brief carried forward

The lettering in round 06 felt too wide and its repeated stone fill looked paneled. The next comparison uses whole generated stone letters, tighter proportions, equal-height and taller statue-A layouts, and a restrained color study. Old references 28, 30, 32 are retained unchanged. Studies 34/36 remain the chosen statue-A sources. Black app icons are the only active icon family in this round; earlier non-black candidates remain archived.

28 and 30 use the same serif outlines; their material and statue treatments differ. The new gallery makes that distinction explicit.

Every direction has equal/tall A, signal/monochrome/oxide foundation, and ink/light appearance variants. The light studies tone the sculpture and carved letters toward charcoal against cool white for stronger separation; they do not simply place pale stone on pale cream. Letter aspect ratios are preserved. The native flat type and new generated stone treatments are separate comparisons.

The review includes six black-background app icons: statue 34 or 36 with no foundation, a stone foundation, or a signal foundation. ## Stone and inscription provenance

The raw image-generation outputs in `generated/` are preserved. They were produced with the built-in Image Generation tool using the previous statue/material studies as references; exact prompts and generation metadata are in `prompts/`. The tool returned RGB checkerboard backgrounds, so the compositing assets use native Photoshop subject extraction with true transparent backgrounds. Raw generations are not suitable as transparent exports.

The family-A shape comes from preserved copies of the selected 34/36 transparent images. Ink studies retain the source color; light studies apply the documented charcoal treatment. New STIR letters 48/49/52/53 are generated interpretations; no claim is made that they are the original artist's actual work. Existing native typography is used for 46/47/50/51. The old repeated/mirrored stone-fill construction from 06 is not used for the new sculpted letters.

ONENESS is a hand-traced recreation of the narrow, uneven inscription photographed on DSCF2954. `build/oneness-photo-detail.png` preserves the enlarged original reference; `build/oneness-original-paths.json` and SVG preserve the trace. It is not an exact pixel extraction or a substituted system font. Its size and position remain on the right end of the extended foundation.

The continuous base comes from the photo-style foundation in study 35, with native color toning. The palette choices are signal #F05A3C, restrained monochrome, and muted oxide #A45B42. They are design comparisons; this round does not change Astir's production palette or map pins. The map-icon discussion is retained as a later identity-alignment consideration after a direction is chosen.

## Exports and preservation

Canonical transparent wordmark PNGs are 1600×764. The matching SVG compositions refer to source images in this folder; keep the folder structure together. Use the full downloadable bundle for editable compositions, or the individual PNG button for a portable flattened asset. No proprietary font files are bundled. A standalone SVG embedding helper is included under `build/`.

`qa/splash-grid-ink.png` and `qa/splash-grid-light.png` are native-rendered iPhone composition sheets, useful without opening the interactive page. They are proposed design mockups, not screenshots from a running iOS build.
