
## R — Earth Observatory

```text
Globe: genuinely photorealistic daylight Earth seen from low orbit, realistic navy ocean, natural olive/tan Europe and North Africa, sparse soft cloud systems, delicate amber atmospheric rim; cinematic but not oversharpened.
Pin: a broad, short-shouldered location marker with a large circular opening and thick body, made from frosted coral-orange glass. Use satin diffusion and soft refraction rather than mirror gloss.
Wordmark: near-black smoked glass, restrained highlights.
Mood: premium scientific instrument meets editorial travel identity.
```

```text

Wordmark: flat charcoal ink or matte black ceramic, proper and crisp.
Mood: tactile museum map, quiet and timeless.
```
