# REC-214 wider-pin prompts

Tool path: built-in image generation. The user's Direction J image was used as
the edit target for N–Q.

## Shared edit specification

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit
Asset type: square iOS app icon concept
Input images: Image 1 is the edit target.
Primary request: Change only the orange liquid-glass map pin silhouette. Preserve the entire source image pixel-for-pixel in concept everywhere else: the exact black glossy serif wordmark reading "rec.me", letterforms, period, spacing, alignment, realistic Earth half-dome, continents, clouds, horizon, peach background, lighting, reflections, colors, camera, crop, and 1:1 composition. The pin must remain centered above the period and its bottom tip must point precisely into the center of that same black period.
Materials/textures: keep the existing translucent coral-orange refractive liquid glass with realistic highlights, caustics, refraction, and a circular opening.
Constraints: edit only the pin; do not alter the wordmark or period; do not alter the globe or background; do not add objects or text; exact text remains "rec.me"; no watermark.
```

## Direction N — Wide classic

```text
Pin option N — Wide Classic: Make the pin about 40% wider through its head and shoulders, with a broader circular glass ring, a thick substantial body, and a shorter taper. The sides should stay full and convex for most of the length, narrowing only near the bottom. It should feel sturdy, iconic, and clearly legible at small app-icon sizes, never needle-thin.
```

## Direction O — Rounded teardrop

```text
Pin option O — Rounded Teardrop: Replace the narrow pin with a plump, inflated teardrop marker. Use broad rounded shoulders, a generous circular opening, and a smooth thick glass body that tapers late into a short precise point. The silhouette should feel soft, premium, and liquid, with noticeably more visual mass than the source.
```

## Direction P — Compact badge

```text
Pin option P — Compact Badge: Replace the narrow pin with a bold compact marker: an oversized round glass badge head with a circular opening, joined to a short wide triangular/trapezoidal lower body that finishes at the period. Keep the lower section broad until close to the tip. The silhouette should feel architectural, confident, and app-icon legible.
```

## Direction Q — Organic pebble

```text
Pin option Q — Organic Pebble: Replace the narrow pin with a wide softly organic liquid marker. Use a slightly irregular inflated-pebble head around the circular opening, broad shoulders, and a flowing thick lower body that bends very subtly before resolving into the centered tip. It may feel hand-shaped and liquid but must still read instantly as a location pin.
```
