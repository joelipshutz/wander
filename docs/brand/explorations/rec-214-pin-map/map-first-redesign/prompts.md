Tool path: built-in image generation.

References:

- Image 1: user-supplied `rec.me` wordmark crop, typography reference only.

## Shared specification

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: square iOS app icon concept
Input images: Image 1 is the typography reference only. Reproduce the exact visual character of its lowercase "rec.me" wordmark: the same dignified black book-serif letterforms, proportions, stroke contrast, compact spacing, and period. Ignore the orange pin fragment in Image 1. Image 2 is the rec.me product-map style reference only: warm off-white map, pale blue water, muted mint/olive parks, light gray roads, terracotta-outlined place markers.
Primary request: create a complete map-first redesign of the rec.me app icon. The map must fill the entire square canvas edge to edge, viewed top-down or slightly folded like a premium navigation map. Show recognizable land, streets or paths, water where appropriate, and multiple rec.me place pins. Overlay the exact wordmark "rec.me" prominently in the type style from Image 1.
Design intent: inspired by the immediate map readability and layered color clarity of modern first-party navigation icons, but original to rec.me and not a copy of any Apple Maps artwork or icon.
Constraints: exact text "rec.me" only; no other labels, street names, highway shields, numbers, Apple logos, compass labels, UI cards, globe, planet horizon, outer border, baked rounded-square mask, watermark, liquid glass, or photoreal satellite imagery. Strong silhouette and wordmark readability at 87 px.
```

## AH — Neighborhood Grid

```text

Use four compact circular rec.me pins with terracotta rings and simple dark centers, distributed asymmetrically.
Wordmark placement: large near-black "rec.me" centered across the middle over a subtle warm-ivory clear zone in the map; no box or pill behind it.
Style: crisp flat vector-like cartography with gentle paper texture and minimal depth.
```
