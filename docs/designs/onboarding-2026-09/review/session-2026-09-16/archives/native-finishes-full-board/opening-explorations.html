What to compare

All three use the same undistorted monospaced caps, eight flips in every cell over 1.5 seconds, three permanently visible rows, fixed hinges and a small landing rebound. Blank cells and repeated letters also flip. Light mode uses black faces; dark mode uses white or off-white faces, with Signal letters in both. Four light haptic taps accompany each opening flutter in Swift; the physical sensation needs an iPhone review. The opening slides into Places; only the upper UI slides from Places to People while the lower board stays put and flips. All three use the real Hotchkiss Park preview and the same account flow.
