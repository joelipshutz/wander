## Established direction

The August strategy describes Astir as a **modern neighborhood cultural institution**, beginning with Ocean Park. Its job is to help people experience life more richly; its enemy is monotony and the feeling of failing to experience time. The initial wedge is the LA experience-seeker drawn to art, wellness, novelty, and mindfulness, with limited time and a strong sense of distance.

The later social-content conversation makes the emotional center more explicit: **culture in motion; connection, togetherness, oneness; people doing things together and knowing their neighbors**. Content should feel like something Joe and Ryan would stop for and send one another. The suggested visual world is recognizably Ocean Park or beautiful nature, with some strangeness or intrigue. Alan Watts was explicitly prioritized, alongside philosophy, poetry, presence, music, play, and community.

Saved taste rules from the reviewed IG session:

- A thought should register immediately and have an obvious use.
- Be confident, concise, and occasionally irreverent.
- Lead with connection, presence, shared experiences, nature, music, and curiosity.
- Avoid a meditation-app voice, preachiness, ornate phrasing, and romance that swallows the community idea.
- “Find out” was explicitly liked as a CTA.
- Preserve source wording and a complete thought when quoting.

For onboarding, these imply a short emotional headline followed by a plain account of what the person gets. That implication is a recommendation, not a previously approved brand rule.
