## After a home event, when should exact-address access end?

We already settled when the address is revealed before the event. This decides what guests can see afterward, while the event and its memories remain in Astir.

- **A — Recommended:** Hide the exact address 24 hours after the event ends. Everyone returns to the approximate location. Astir can adjust this cutoff for each event in the console.
- **B:** Recorded attendees keep the exact address. Confirmed guests who did not attend lose it when the event ends.
- **C:** All confirmed guests keep the exact address indefinitely.
- **D:** Tell me your preferred rule.

I recommend **A** because guests can still use the address immediately afterward without leaving a home's address permanently available through an old event.

Reply **35A**, **35B**, **35C**, or **35D** with your rule. These choices concern future access; they cannot erase an address someone already saw.
