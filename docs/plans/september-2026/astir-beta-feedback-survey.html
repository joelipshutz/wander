Astir beta feedback survey

Thanks for trying Astir. We want the honest version, including what felt confusing, broken or unnecessary. Answer about the build you used. Choose “not tried” where relevant and skip optional questions. This is a 30-question beta survey; allow roughly 10–15 minutes, with timing to be checked in a pilot. Your feedback will guide app improvements. Contact details and follow-up are optional. Completing this survey does not subscribe you to a newsletter.

30 questions / Multiple choice, true-or-false style and free text

Open the Google Form draft. All 30 questions are built and a response spreadsheet is linked. The form is unpublished for review and a two-person pilot; no invitations have been sent.
